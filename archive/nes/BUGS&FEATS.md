# FEATURES
- [ ] **NES steadier pacing, ideas from the GB work (2026-11-02; for after the `dmg-exact` merge, not started, nothing measured on the current ROM).** The GB's big gain was not waiting a whole frame after a slightly late pass. `src/main.c` has the same flaw: its comment says "only if none passed", but GBDK's `vsync` (disassembled from the ROM at `$C357`) flushes the shadow attributes, sets the flag in `__oam_valid_display_on`, and always waits for the next `sys_time` tick. So a 1.01-frame pass costs 2.
  - **Do not copy the GB fix.** It needs a second OAM page (256 aligned bytes; NES RAM has 3 free), and skipping `vsync()` loses the deferred VBL/LCD handlers and the OAM DMA arming. It would gain little anyway: late frames at fast were 1.0-3.3% (2026-10-30, `PROGRESS.md` "Row render and pacing, part 2"), against 61% of passes on the GB before its fix.
  - **Step 1, measure:** late frames on the current ROM, as those numbers are old and later features cost cycles (`tests/perf_frame_trace.mjs`, `tests/perf_exp.ps1`). If they are still near the old figures, the game may not need any of this. Fast flight stays at 1.5 px/frame: the gain is steadier motion, not speed.
  - **Idea A, medium risk: work during the idle wait.** Repeat all of `vsync()`'s steps, but generate river rows while waiting for the tick instead of spinning. Late passes were mostly those generating 3-4 rows, and row generation should not touch OAM, which must stay unchanged once the flag is set. Could remove most of the remaining late frames. Must fit the 3 free bytes of RAM. Check how the NMI treats the flag: only read so far from the flag's name and `AGENTS.md`'s "arms OAM DMA".
  - **Idea B, largest gain, high risk: use the HUD split wait.** The NMI spins through scanlines 0-29, ~3.2k of 29.8k cycles, about 11% of every frame. It could end on a sprite-0 hit and do bounded work meanwhile (`.tim_emulation` hook; `PROGRESS.md`, same entry). Changes how GBDK's split works and needs a hardware test.
  - Fix the misleading comment in `src/main.c` either way; that changes no ROM bytes.
